# Dala Suite

A free After Effects panel for edits: face tracking with Cut to F, offline auto captions
(Tamil, English, Tanglish, Hinglish), beat markers from the drums, vocal stems, an ease graph,
presets and layer tools.

**Website and download: https://dala-suite.vercel.app**

This repository only hosts the releases. Get the newest version from
[Releases](https://github.com/roktemp29/dala-suite/releases/latest): download the `.zxp`
attached to the release.

## Install

1. Download `DalaSuite-vX.Y.Z.zxp` from the latest release.
2. Close After Effects and install the `.zxp` with a ZXP installer
   (aescripts + aeplugins ZXP Installer, or Anastasiy's Extension Manager).
3. In After Effects: **Edit > Preferences > Scripting & Expressions >**
   **Allow Scripts to Write Files and Access Network**.
4. Restart After Effects and open **Window > Extensions > Dala Suite**.

Requirements: Windows 10 / 11 (64-bit), After Effects 2020 or newer.

## Updating

Download the newest `.zxp` and install it over the old one. Your Layer Setups, curves,
speech engine and models are kept.

## Bugs and questions

Open an [issue](https://github.com/roktemp29/dala-suite/issues) with your After Effects
version and what you clicked.
